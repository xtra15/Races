Begin Your Saga

Get Started

The path is set before you. Watch, then walk it — a race first, and a trio of classes to carry.

I

Claim your race

Your blood is given, never chosen — but it is yours to claim. Run claimrace in game to receive the one race dealt to you by the fates. Each race carries a roll of blessings and a measure of curses; see them all in the Races ledger.

II

Claim your classes

Command claimclass to draw three classes, each from a different calling (Warrior, Specialist, Adept, and so on). Your callings shape how you fight and endure. Browse every path in the Classes roll.

III

Forge your tools

Arm your legend with the enchants this server honours, then measure your lot against the greatest fated builds the fates allow.
